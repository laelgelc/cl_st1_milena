    Summary

    This is the first riddle that helped kick-start my creative writing from the beginning. Back in September 2020, during the first week of my creative writing class at university, I was assigned to write at least five pages of an original story to present to the class. After a night of a panic attack and then sitting down with my laptop for the next few days to think up a new story, I wrote down what would be my first draft of my stories featuring Alystair and Elianore, and it was called “Till the Candle Burns Out”. This draft also included the addition of a short riddle that Elianore quickly devised to entertain Alystair before he drifted off to sleep; this rather poetic piece helps encompass Alystair and Elianore’s relationship in my opinion, that no matter what may be going on or how much time as passed, each one of them will stand by the other to provide light and warmth with their presence. This was only my first story I ever presented to the class, but after the positive feedback and encouragement to expand upon the story and feature more of these two characters, this of course led to me coming up with more ideas and to write more stories for Alystair and Elianore.

    I hope you will enjoy this riddle for what it is.
