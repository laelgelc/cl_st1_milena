    Summary

    There have been wars between humans and humans. There have been wars between humans and the shadows of the darkness. There have been wars between humans and gods and between gods and shadows and between gods and gods. There have been wars in which no one won and everyone lost.
    Now, there is a war on the horizon that looks like it might be all of the above.

     

    Seon has been dead for fifty years. When he is revived, he comes back as a ghost - one that has memories, but no historical ones; he knows not who he was in life or why he died, but he knows how to fight. He knows things like the names of cities and why they were at war. Most importantly, he knows the two children he found dying on his grave were the most crutial part of his new life. They might be the ones to save the world now, in between new drugs made of the blood of lingerers - dark underworld beings that were like shadows and tied themselves to the human realm by feeding on the plants of light that were human souls - and gods running amok in the human realm, gods that tried to help and the difficulty of not knowing who to trust.
