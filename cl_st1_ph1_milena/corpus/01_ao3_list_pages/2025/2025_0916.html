Sparks of pain pricked through his numb fingers as he twisted, trying to create enough give in his bindings to slip free. Nothing loosened.

    Another voice spoke, softly enough that he couldn’t make out the words. In answer, the first voice came again.

    “Well it’s not my problem. Glad I’m not going to be in your shoes when Matthias finds out you have him.”
