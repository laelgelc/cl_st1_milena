    An unlikely event caused all the life on earth to die by knocking it off the solar system, for much more than a millenia of years, the earth wandered across the cold and extensive space void, going through various geographic (And other types) of changes.

    But after travelling enough... It reached a solar system that orbited around a star, not just any star, but a special one, and after staying in the goldilocks zone (Safe zone of the orbit of a star), the seed of life was planted, and it grew, and grew... The life in this earth could now explore different paths now with the presence of magic, allowing life to explore many more possibilities to be shaped in ways that it never could take on earth, and do many things that it could never do before
    (This is the general lore about the settling of the story).
