    Lucas Creams isn’t your typical CEO.
    At 32, he’s the reluctant heir to a brutal criminal empire hidden behind the luxury of Creams Tower. To wear the crown, you have to kill for it. Lucas has—and it haunts him every night.

    On paper, he’s all suits and silence. But beneath? A secret Instagram account where he teases dark romance lovers as “Helmet Daddy”—always masked, always in control.
