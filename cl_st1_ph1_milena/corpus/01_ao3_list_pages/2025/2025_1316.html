    Lillianne Ranger has loved Atlas Oz’bin for almost a decade.
    Seven confessions. Seven silences.
    Every year, every loop, she swears she’ll let go—only to fall right back into him. He’s her first friend, her first tether, her first ruin. But when love keeps returning only to leave her hollow, how much of herself can she lose before there’s nothing left?
    This is a story about a girl who loved too long, about grief disguised as hope, about what it means to survive when the person you built your world around walks away. And about the unexpected friendship that kept her from shattering completely.

    It began in ruin. And maybe in ruin, it must end.
