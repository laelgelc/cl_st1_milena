    Summary

    Clara Walker is a bright, inventive young girl living in Vigrid, Estreanor's capital. Taught at the kingdom’s most prestigious academy and with the power of technomancy at her fingertips, it was inevitable that adventure would come calling her name.

    Alongside her beloved friend Yganea Ironheart, an elfkin and monk, the two of them are joined by three others—Adrienne the Priestess, Filbert the Barbarian, and Herman the Ranger—as they set out to find a long lost piece of history…

    Only for catastrophe to strike, changing the course of their destiny forever.

    Barely escaping with their lives, they return to Vigrid and discover there’s so much to their encounter than they could have ever imagined. And now they must pursue the past’s shadow, joined by unlikely friends, in hopes of untangling history’s threads and putting this darkness to rest once and for all.
