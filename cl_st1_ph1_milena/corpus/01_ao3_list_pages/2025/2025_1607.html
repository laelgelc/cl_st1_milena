The Koleksi is an anthology (a compilation of texts of a variety of forms) which was originally written in the Scribble of Ikadus and Vania Lake linguistics. The texts encompass instructions, stories, prophecies, and more. The earliest and considered to be the first read of the Collection is the Script of Creation ("Kreate" in Ikadusian).
