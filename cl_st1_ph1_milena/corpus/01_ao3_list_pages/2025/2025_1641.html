    Summary

    Lillian’s life is a tedious cycle of thankless tasks and dismissive colleagues, leaving her craving anything but the mundane. A desperate delve into an ancient grimoire leads to an accidental summoning. Enter Areth, a captivating Incubus, who sees past Lillian’s drab existence to her deepest, most guarded desires. He offers her everything she's ever wanted – respect, power, and a thrilling escape from anonymity – but at the ultimate cost: her soul. As Areth tempts her with promises of exquisite fulfilment and a world where she is truly seen, Lillian must choose between a lifetime of quiet desperation and a dangerous, intoxicating deal with a demon who intends to make her scream his name.
