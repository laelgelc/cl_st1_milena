    But he sees what I really am - a vessel, aching to be filled.

    In a house of secrets, with a father who worships power and a stranger who teaches me how to burn, I learn that shame is sacred and surrender is survival.

    Sanctum: Initiation is a slow-burning gospel of ritual, devotion, and filthy transformation, where every touch is prayer, every bruise a benediction, and the body is just another altar to be blessed, broken, and claimed.
