    Summary

    Concept Note:a ghost of a tear is a digitally-native narrative project that explores the blurred boundaries between virtual and physical intimacy. Entirely conceptualized, written, and "lived" online, it interrogates the idea that digital relationships are less real than their in-person counterparts. Rather than rejecting the ephemeral nature of the internet, it seeks to physicalize the digital — not by leaving the digital space, but by making it feel as tangible, visceral, and emotionally weighty as the real world.

    This project was aided in workskins by La_Temperanza and TrojanTeapot. The project uses anchor links to guide the reader through as many choices as possible and be encouraged to sit with each piece of text rather than skim through the entire work. The aim of this method is help readers sit with these characters and begin to question what it means to value a relationship.

    We live so much of our lives online — yet our culture still treats those connections as shallow. a ghost of a tear is a small and silent rebellion against that. It says: what happens in the digital is real. What we feel through a screen still counts.
