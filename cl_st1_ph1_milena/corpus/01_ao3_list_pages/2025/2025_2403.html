    Summary

    In 1980, Sam Deckard was transformed by the evil scientist Dr Synergy into the vicious Spyderite. To save him from persecution in their home in the spaceborne colony of Spotlight City, his friend Solar Girl and her partner/his mother Moonshine perform a ritual to transport him to a safer world: the shadow-city of Grimshaw, hidden away in the depths of the Kingdom of Inglenook's Blackwood Forest, in the Other Realms.

    Now, the year is 1981, and this is his story.
