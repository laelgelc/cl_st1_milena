    Wren Holloway inherits her late mother’s isolated coastal house and slowly realizes she's not alone in it. Adrian Bennett kept that house standing through her father’s death and her mother’s last winter. He listened to every story. He looked at every photograph. He did not leave.

    He does not rush her. He studies her. He touches her while she sleeps. He rearranges her days until the house and Wren belong to him.

    A dark, claustrophobic story of patience, grief, and the softest kind of ownership.
