    Summary

    The collective isn't hidden anymore.

    Well. Mostly.

    With the end of high school approaching, Crystal and the others are learning that discovering each other was only the beginning. There are exams to survive, college subjects to choose, family relationships to rebuild, boundaries to negotiate, and the increasingly complicated matter of River—who somehow managed to become important to more than one person in the system.

    Then summer brings an invitation to Belgium.

    A holiday with River means meeting their family, returning to the country they left behind, navigating an unfamiliar place as a system, and discovering that River's connection to the collective may stretch further into their childhood than anyone realised.

    And when they return home, college is waiting.

    New classrooms. New people. New rules. New decisions about who gets to know.

    The body is still sixteen.

    There are still names they don't know.

    But this time, they're not trying to uncover the whole collective.

    They're trying to build a life that belongs to all of them.
