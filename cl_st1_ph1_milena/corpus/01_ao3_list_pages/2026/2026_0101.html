    Summary

    Life should’ve been looking up for Markus when he finished his term of service. No more looking over his own shoulder or anything! He had peace, a degree, and options now! All his life was ahead of him!

    …

    Kinda.

    Life had decided that path was entirely too easy for Markus. It thought that he really needed to put his skills to the test. Thus after one fatefully average night of sleep, he found himself waking in a fantastical world and set to act as one of it many ‘heroes’. Great…

    Almost exactly a year following his induction to the world, Markus was sent to cover the evacuation of a costal trade city by the name of Krenos. His mission was to match and capture Bethelmar, a demoness princess that had no feats to speak of. Peachy. Fantastic. No feats should mean easy right?

    If Bethelmar didn’t kill him, Markus was going to kill whoever was responsible for intel.
