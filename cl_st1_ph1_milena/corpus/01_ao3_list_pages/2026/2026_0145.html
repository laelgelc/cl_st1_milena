    🏒 Across the Blue Line

    Cody Michaud has everything a rookie could want: a Cup ring, a spot on the Vancouver Lynxes’ defensive line, and a city that adores him. But behind the swagger and tattoos, the twenty-one-year-old golden boy is still trying to figure out if he truly belongs in the dynasty he stumbled into.

    Then there’s Mikko Korhonen.

    Seattle’s star sniper. Finland’s Ice Prince.
    Cody’s assignment on the ice - and the one player he’s been low-key obsessed with since junior hockey.

    Their first clash is supposed to be routine: defense versus offense, shut him down, don’t overthink it.
    But one clipped word in Mikko’s thick Finnish accent, one steady stare across the ice, and Cody knows he’s already in trouble.

    Because rivalry is easy.
    Wanting your rival?
    That’s a whole different game.
