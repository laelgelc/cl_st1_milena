    He wasn't forced.
    He consented-fully, deliberately... and with just enough ignorance.

    He learned quickly that eternity isn't a reward. It's repetition. That blood feeds the body, but doesn't always fill the hollow beneath it. That pacts don't break. They carry through.

    This man-who once had three names and now pretends he only needs one-decided the safest way to survive was not to belong. Not to bring food home. Not to say certain words. Not to grow attached to anything that breathes.

    For centuries, it worked.

    Until the world, with the patience only inevitability has, offered him a different choice.

    And every fable knows there are decisions that don't look dangerous... until you say them out loud.

    This is the story of a vampire who thought he understood the cost of eternity.
    Until the world-through blood, through strangers, through bonds he never meant to form-offered him a different choice.
