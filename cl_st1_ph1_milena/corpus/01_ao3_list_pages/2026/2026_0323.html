    Summary

    "Where the hell are you this time?"

    A bit tired, not altogether rude, but all the same expectant of an answer.

    And yeah, Roni was used to that sort of thing. He was used to being the cause of exasperated gazes and slow exhales through the nose, and the knowledge that everything he tried always seemed to feel ever so slightly fallen short. Except for this. These nights. These wild moments chasing streaks of pleasure.

    Because if he were a good time...then for once, for once...he was good enough.
