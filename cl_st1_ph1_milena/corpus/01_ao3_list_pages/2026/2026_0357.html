    Perseus is sent to bring a king Medusa’s head. Beyond the edge of his map, he finds three sisters keeping house, a woman whose gaze is dangerous, and a reason to question the task.

    Coming home without the head is only the beginning. Polydectes still wants Danaë, the gods have their own accounts to defend, and a painter has been ordered to show a victory that never happened.
