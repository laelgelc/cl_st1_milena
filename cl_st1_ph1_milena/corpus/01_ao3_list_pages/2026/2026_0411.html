    Summary

    Eirian Soliel was raised to rule, but her entire life is derailed when her stepmother marries her off sight unseen. Eirian finds herself into a forced union with a dying lord, trapped in a dying estate. A place that hasn't held a celebration in a hundred years, and no one there will tell her why.

    But she quickly realizes she doesn't need them to. The moment she lays eyes on the estate, she sees it: something dark clinging to the stones, the soldiers, the very air— a slow poison no healer has ever named.

    And the people living it have no idea it's even there.

    Her husband, Lord Ye Chenzhou, has spent his entire life at the Camelia. Sickly and weak, he still managed to lead the Crimson Army, but he knows his time is short. To protect his beloved home, he searched for a wife capable of defending it and finds Eirian. Chenhzou has no idea what's eating away at the Camelia, and Eirian has no idea why someone would target this place, but somewhere beneath the black cliffs is the truth of what's been killing this place for generations — and who wanted it dead.
