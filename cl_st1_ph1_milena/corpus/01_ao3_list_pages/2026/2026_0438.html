    **The Work in Common**

    Australia, 2084. The systems meant to keep the country running are sophisticated, but they do not always see what people know. A grain mill reports that it is working while it stands still. A map misses the way water moves. Skills learned over a lifetime go unrecognised because they do not fit a job description.

    Across seven regions, farmers, repairers, teachers, carers and young people begin connecting what they know. Their work brings them up against rules about who may act, what may be shared and how long a community can afford to wait for permission. As fire, rain and illness test those rules, they must decide which knowledge to trust and which responsibilities to pass on.

    *The Work in Common* imagines a capable but imperfect future. It is a novel about the value of work that wages cannot fully measure, and about what becomes possible when people are allowed to learn from one another.
