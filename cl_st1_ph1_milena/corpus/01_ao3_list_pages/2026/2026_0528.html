    Summary

    ” The first storm burned the ocean, drying out the seas. The second storm burned the land, and that all that had breathed. The third and final shall burn and purify our lives, leaving all life…behind.”

    An original work set on a post-apocalyptic Earth, inspired very loosely with Christian mythology.

    Hebea, a glorified young teenage girl, struggles to understand the human concepts of love, kindness, and compassion. As she encounters the remnant of humanity, the people she meets begin to challenge everything she though she understood about herself and the world.

    In a dying world, Hebea searches for answers to questions she was never taught how to ask.
