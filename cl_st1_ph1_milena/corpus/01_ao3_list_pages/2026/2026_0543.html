He was a pathetic, soft, greasy loser, and I liked to tease him: to be the prettiest thing in his house and let him look, knowing he'd never get to touch it. I thought that made me the one with the power. I never wondered what he did with all that wanting, alone in the basement, building a copy of me a piece at a time. My hair. My voice. My face. My body. A soft, obedient mouth that would never do what mine did and tell him no.
