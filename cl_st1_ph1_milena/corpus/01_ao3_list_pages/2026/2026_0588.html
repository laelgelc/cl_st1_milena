    Now, in a new city and surrounded by people who know almost nothing about him, Nicholas has the chance to start over.

    Or at least that is what he wants to believe.

    Because some pasts do not disappear just because no one knows the story. And the harder Nicholas tries to keep his distance, the more difficult it becomes to stop new people from slipping past the walls he spent so long building.
