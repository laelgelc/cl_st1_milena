    Summary

    The lands of Wylen have gone barren, plagued by famine and the terrifying whispers of 'the Gods' Forsakening.' Armed with his father’s heavy warrior sword, Prince Kieran leaves the safety of the palace walls on a desperate quest to find the priests who abandoned them.

    But the wasteland is unforgiving. Navigating a dying world with nothing but a novice's sword skills and a strange trinket a pickpocket mysteriously shoved into his trousers, Kieran will have to harden his soft, royal resolve if he wants to survive the road ahead.
