    Summary

    It's summer time at Wild Rose Ranch, which means for Ginny, her aunt and uncle who live states away in New York are coming to visit, bringing along her cousins EJ and Maddie. EJ's fine, he's the same age as her younger twin brothers, and the three of them get along great. Maddie however, is the worst! A true city girl and the antithesis to Ginny. The two girls could not get along if their lives depended on it. Due to current family circumstances, the spare bedroom Maddie usually uses when she comes to stay is occupied, which means as the one closest in age to her visiting cousin, the best option is for Maddie to stay in Ginny's room with her. Ginny doesn't take this news well.
