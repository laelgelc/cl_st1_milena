In reality, Miles is an ultra-wealthy, cold-blooded genius operating an offshore trading portfolio from his secluded estate, enforcing his true power as the feared shadow vigilante known as "The Reaper."

    Talia, traumatized and hunted, is the daughter of ruthless syndicate boss Victor Vance. Desperate for escape, she finds sanctuary in Miles’s quiet home. Though subtle clues hint he is far more dangerous than he lets on, her desperate need for protection buries her suspicion. Miles is her safe harbor.

    When Victor Vance’s empire crumbles, he forces Talia back into his fortified safehouse to trade her into an arranged political marriage. Playing the helpless, worried boyfriend, Miles drops her off at her father's gates.

    The second she’s out of sight, the timid facade dies. Hacking into the safehouse’s security feed, Miles watches her every move through high-definition night vision.
