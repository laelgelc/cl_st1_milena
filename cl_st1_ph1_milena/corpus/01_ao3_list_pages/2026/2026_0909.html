    Summary

    An ongoing cycle of original philosophical fiction told through treatises, correspondences, and archival fragments. Within the Abyssal Mind, multiple voices rise, fracture, converge, and assume different registers of thought: some govern through twilight, some reckon through cold structure, some sever through shadow, and others remain unnamed, waiting their turn to speak. Through these shifting documents, the work explores sovereignty, false authority, kinship, endurance, identity, corruption, and the cost of preserving the self without surrendering to the forces that deform it. Less a conventional narrative than an evolving manifesto, it reads as the surviving literature of an inner empire still being written.
