    Artemis—a lustful, arrogant noble will finally have to learn her place in the world as it quickly comes crumbling down.

    "In this world there are many kinds of people, but most fall into 2 categories. Rich or poor, winners or losers, and that dictates your fate. Whether you end up a servant or having servants, begging on the streets, or steering clear of beggars, being obedient towards a man who doesn't show you off or a man who does, the list could go on. Yet we’re still humans, weak vessels which are used for whomever we ‘choose’ or don’t choose. No one is truly free. Freedom is a lie served by those with the most power to drive the weak links to serve them. Promises that could never be fulfilled. Truly, what a pathetic life we live."
