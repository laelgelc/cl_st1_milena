    When a scattered series of unexplained deaths begins to suggest an impossible pattern, police turn to academic historian Maria Vale for help. The connection between the victims is almost nonexistent: fleeting encounters, forgotten lectures, weak institutional links and coincidences that collapse the moment investigators examine them. At the centre stands Professor Adrian Vey, a brilliant specialist in quantum cognition whose theories of contextual identity, observer effects and psychological partitioning may conceal something far darker.

    As Maria reconstructs the murders, she discovers an abandoned behavioural experiment called JANUS, a network of academics, consultants and police systems designed to study how observers manufacture certainty from ambiguous information. But somebody is still running the experiment—and Maria has become its newest subject.

    Part intellectual thriller, part procedural mystery, The Observer Effect explores murder without motive, weaponised uncertainty, institutional manipulation and the terrifying possibility that the investigation itself has been deliberately engineered.
