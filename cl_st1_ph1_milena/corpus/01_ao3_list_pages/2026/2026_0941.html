    Summary

    “Doesn’t the bible preach about loving all? I don’t know, but it sounds like bullshit to me that god has something against love.”

    For Cora, loving the ocean—and loving girls—has always been as simple and natural as breathing. For Elenore, trapped beneath the weight of her family’s rigid expectations and fears of "sin," love is a quiet, terrifying confession spoken into the dark.

    Brought together by a night walk on the beach and a shared art project, their late-night study sessions slowly turn into stolen glances, shared headphones, and warm, quiet kisses. But as summer fades and the real world closes in, loving each other in secret might not be enough to save them from the lives waiting for them back on land.
