    Summary

    Hasthinas Maison is a global fashion empire, dominating everything from haute couture and cosmetics to fine jewelry. Its iconic Queen of the Night emblem consistently reigns over prestigious runways in Paris, Milan, New York, Tokyo, and Jakarta.

    This massive empire wasn't built by a corporate strategist, but by Mr. Danendra—a humble tailor who started with a small boutique and a single old sewing machine, driven by the belief that clothing is a reflection of one's identity.

    Now in the twilight of his life, Mr. Danendra faces his ultimate question: Who is truly worthy of inheriting Hasthinas Maison?
