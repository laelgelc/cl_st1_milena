    Summary

    We all have a people pleaser in our life, we can’t understand why are they like this because they never share their life with us.
    Chris told Nancy he loved her a while ago, in two days. Nancy didn’t even know she was getting love-bombed.
    Her friends kept encouraging her into solutions but Nancy couldn’t do it. Her friends were more brave, not concerned.
    Chris also says she’s too loud, too sensitive, too fat, and too difficult. There is a couple of things she isn’t according to Chris; Not as sexy as his ex, not as funny as his ex, not as slim as his ex and not as obedient as his ex.
    Nancy has learned to live with contradictions. Until one late-night conversation with a stranger makes her wonder if maybe love isn’t supposed to hurt. She’s just too brainwashed by Chris to think about that. If it’s true or not, or if Maverick is just a creepy stranger she met from an app or if he’s a bot.
