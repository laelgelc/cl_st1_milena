    Summary

    "19 is the new 13. an odd age- liminal, psychologically daunting... an extension of our 13-year-old selves we never left behind, and simultaneously a foreshadowment of the adults we will be."

    a chronological collection of raw, unedited notes app prose and stream of consciousness thoughts that i've captured between the ages of 14 to currently (19). exploring the suffocating weight of the patriarchy, the performance of youth, and the absolute, terrifying necessity of freedom as i find myself.

    written because my past versions knew me better than i do now, and because i like my pages wrote in and read.
