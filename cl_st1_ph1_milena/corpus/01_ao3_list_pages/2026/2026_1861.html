    one cold morning in asteria, kevin kastello--the man, the myth and the legend himself--arose from his bed and glanced out the window. outside the glass he found himself looking upon a blanket of frothy, fluffy, white- 
    "SNOWW!!"
    he exclaimed, slipping on some shoes that were lying around and shoving the door open. with childlike joy, kevin continues his voyage through the icy sea, his shoes crunching beneath him. 
    "Roman- argh!"
    a familiar voice suddenly rang in dear kevin's ears. he whipped his head around to find cayden and roman draped in winter attire, snowballs in hand. roman had thrown a... yellow snowball at cayden, who was now begrudgingly brushing off the remnants off his luxurious fur coat. kevin chuckles, watching his curly haired friend blissfully twirl around, doing his silly little victory dance. 
    "i'm going to kill you!"
    grumbles cayden, but roman--as flippant as ever--spots kevin and starts waddling towards him. 
    "kevin! can you be on my side this time? pwease?"
    he asks, snowflakes clinging to his eyelashes. 
    "where's nikolai?" 
    "probably still sleeping.."
