    Riley had never thought he’d go over the point of no return, the blood on his hands making the adrenaline feel that much better…he expected even less that his friend since 8th grade, Ceasar, would march right past it with him without a second thought.

    A whole new book of emotions are brought up when Ceasar starts dressing up as ‘Sally’.
    “Why do you look like that?”
    “Well if we’re doing this I wanna have some fun with it! Besides, there can only be one Final Girl, right?”
    His snickering only brought more heat to Riley’s face. Whatever, so what his best literal partner in crime was cute in a mini skirt? So what he looked even better without anything and his makeup ruined—
    …Oh. Oh that was gonna be a problem.

    Read along to see Riley finally admit his feelings for Ceasar in AND out of a dress, his gay awakening and all!
