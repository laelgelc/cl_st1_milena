    Summary

    Maroon spent his entire life serving Queen Violetta as her mage. He was in love with her, believing that perhaps there was a chance that she could return his feelings someday, even though they were forbidden from ever being together. She made him do many horrible things, yet he convinced himself that they were for the greater good. However, years later, after having been drained of his magic in the completion of creating her supersoldiers that she will use to rule her kingdom with an iron fist, known as the Queen's Angels, he has finally realized that she has been using him all along. Weak and barely able to function, in one desperate attempt to right his wrongs, he decides that he wants to try to stop her...but he may just be too late.

    (Maroon is an original fox character I made from a series I'm writing called The Outcasts! I'm making it for myself, but figured I'd post it on ToyHouse and A03! This is a short story. Think of it as a prequel. It'll have three parts. I dunno how to do original stories here, but I've seen them before, so yeah.)
