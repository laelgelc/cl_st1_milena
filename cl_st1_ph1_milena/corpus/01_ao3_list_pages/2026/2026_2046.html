    Summary

    The age of gods has ended, but their legacy lives on in twelve demigods.

    In the Eonian Republic, twelve vessels carry the magic of the forgotten pantheon, sworn to defend the realm. Noura is one of them—heir to divine power, trained to become a champion of the Republic alongside five others.

    But while the world celebrates her strength, Noura has never trusted the magic within her.

    As war spreads across the provinces and whispers of rebel demigods and a mysterious weapon stir, she must decide whether she is ready to embrace the power—and the duty—she has spent her life longing to escape.

    Because something older than the Republic is awakening, and the first threads of fate are beginning to tighten around them all.
