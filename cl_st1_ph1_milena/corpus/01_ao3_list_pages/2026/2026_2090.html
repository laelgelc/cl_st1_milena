    Amanda Bell was eight years old when she first saw Mr Teeth: a smiling figure in her bedroom with black eyes, too many teeth, and a promise to return when she forgot him. Thirty years later, Amanda is a successful but exhausted professional whose sleep is collapsing, her career is unravelling, and childhood terror has begun bleeding back through mirrors, dreams, and therapy rooms. When regression therapy opens the wrong door, Amanda and psychologist Vivian Shaw discover that Mr Teeth is not a memory, but an ancient predator feeding on unwitnessed fear. To survive, Amanda must confront the child she was, the adults who denied her, and the rooms where silence grows teeth.
