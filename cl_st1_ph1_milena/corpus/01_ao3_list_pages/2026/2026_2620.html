    They're meant to be each other's foils in the ring. As far as Daniel's concerned, that means their fates are intertwined, and he needs to make sure his rookie - Raul, with beautiful eyes and cologne that lingers on the skin - behaves, the way Daniel had to learn when he was younger.

    It's like holding a leash, the lead ever tightening.
