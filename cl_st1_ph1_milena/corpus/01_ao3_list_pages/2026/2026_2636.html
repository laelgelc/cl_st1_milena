    There are two worlds: the world of werewolves, and non-werewolves, many being spell holders.
     
    These worlds collide.

    Einar Knox is a werewolf, the last of his bloodline with no way for it to continue. He spends his days on his own with his cat, spending hours in books and studies, because that's all he can do, be smart and not die.

    Soren Bexley is werewolf assassin who works for his father, Nox Bexley, leader of Lux Noctis, the people with the shared belief that werewolves need to be eradicated for a better world. He chooses to attempt to live up his father's name, but the conflicting views of the ideals outside his own cause that choice to wane.

    Ailith Darius is the right hand to Nox Bexley. It seemed her entire was dedicated to the Lux Noctis ideals. Yet, she'd been planning a revolt with her brother ever since she first heard she was well enough to train.

    Xanythe despises every reprimand she received while being in training. So, then and there she decides she was done. With the lie of being on an assignment she makes her escape. Only to end up on the other end of the said assignments ire. Unprepared and untrained, the blood of a werewolf is now hers. Now there was no returning home.
