    Summary

    She was only supposed to be there for her friends.

    Just moral support—someone to stand safely on the sidelines while they did something bold, something she would never do.

    But the line between watching and wanting is thinner than it seems.

    One hesitant decision leads to another, and what begins in nerves and second thoughts slowly unfolds—step by step—into something else. Curiosity. Courage. A version of herself she’s never quite allowed to exist.

    By the time the camera sees her clearly, she’s starting to see herself the same way.

    And when she finally shares that version of herself with the person she trusts most…

    It doesn’t feel like an ending.

    It feels like a beginning.
