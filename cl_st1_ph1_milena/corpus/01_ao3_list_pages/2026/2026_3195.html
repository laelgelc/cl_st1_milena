    Love is a hungry thing - and Jonathan, Jonathan is full. A vampire stuck in the bureaucracy of high vampire society is called upon. His biological hourglass has reached its last grain of sand as the leash on his neck tightens. They wish to control him - keep him safe, cozy, shacked up with a nice Soulmate to keep his lap warm and his house clean. But when that Soulmate turns out to be a rowdy, messy teenager who barely knows that vampires exist, what does he do? Oh right. Pulls them right into his coven. A prime show of self-restraint. Not like he has a whole handful of other people to tend to, all as messed up as he is. Maybe they were right. Maybe he does need some balance.
