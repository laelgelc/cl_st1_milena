    Summary

    Tye is a sixteen year old that's been living in Arizona for a while. Now it's summer in his small town and not only is it hot as hell, but his mom's pushing him to get a job. That's how he ended up in a rundown ice cream shop with no real business despite it being the perfect weather for something cold. Even with no customers the place is still open somehow? Whatever. He meets his co-worker Ren who's socially awkward as all hell, and hates holding conversation. Throughout the summer, Tye makes it his goal to make this the summer where Ren experiences new things, and offers an escape to his troubling home life. He wants to make the boy smile more often-- that's not weird, right?
